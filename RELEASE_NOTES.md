# v0.1.0-beta.4 — Tape & Tube for Windows

Tape & Tube is a Windows adaptation of [NTSCRT](https://github.com/finnmckenty/NTSCRT) by Finn McKenty. This beta adds a simpler way to choose looks and more export formats.

- **Basic and Expert.** Apply a look in Basic or open the detailed effect and animation controls in Expert. Tape & Tube remembers your mode.
- **Looks and presets.** Basic includes tape wear, antenna ghosting and three NTSCRT originals. Open all 44 original looks and five Screen Loop presets directly from the bundled preset folders.
- **More export formats.** Save images as PNG, JPEG, WebP, TIFF or BMP. Export videos as MP4, MOV with ProRes 422, or WebM with VP9 and Opus. Animated GIF is also available.
- **Components & Credits.** Find project credits, software versions and licenses, and the graphics adapter in dedicated tabs.
- **Smaller Windows package.** The matching source download supplies the NTSCRT source files used to build the effects engine. The installer includes the original presets and license notices.

The shared effects engine requires a graphics adapter with compute shader support. Video export and WebP use FFmpeg with the corresponding encoders.
