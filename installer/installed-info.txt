Tape & Tube - Windows beta

Matching application source: sources\TapeTube-0.1.0-beta.1-source.zip
Other upstream sources and notices: sources\ and licenses\

Windows adaptation of NTSCRT by Finn McKenty:
https://github.com/finnmckenty/NTSCRT

Own code: GPL-3.0-only. Third-party components retain their licenses.

Images require no FFmpeg. For video, add ffmpeg.exe and ffprobe.exe to
runtime\media, or use an existing PATH installation. FFmpeg is not bundled.

Microsoft runtimes: setup checks their loadability and offers official
downloads if missing. The Microsoft installers may require admin rights.

Setup: Inno Setup by Jordan Russell and Martijn Laan, https://jrsoftware.org/
VirtualBox CRTSim export limitation: see licenses\EXPORT_FLICKER_INVESTIGATION.md.
