Tape & Tube is a Windows adaptation of NTSCRT by Finn McKenty.
https://github.com/finnmckenty/NTSCRT

Own application and host integration: GPL-3.0-only.
Third-party components retain their own licenses and attribution.

All seven CRT presets, original notices, license review and matching own
source ZIP are included. Known upstream shader-scope uncertainties are
documented, following the project's adopted upstream-oriented policy.

This beta is unsigned. FFmpeg is optional and is not distributed here.
Required Microsoft runtimes are checked, not silently installed.

VirtualBox: CRTSim exports may contain black frames. This was not
reproduced on the physical host with the same source and preset.
